Privacy Policy

How Piano Tiles Academy handles information when you learn, practice, and play music.

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Developer: AlphaSubZero

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes information handling in Piano Tiles Academy (the “App”), an Android application developed by AlphaSubZero for piano practice, interactive lessons, and MIDI-based musical learning.

The App is designed to support core learning and practice features locally on your device. Features and data handling may change in future versions; when they do, this policy will be updated accordingly.

2. Information handled by the App

  • Learning progress: completed lessons, unlocked chapters, practice results, scores, settings, and other learning preferences may be stored locally so you can resume your studies.
  • Imported MIDI files: when you choose to import a MIDI file through the Android file picker, the App accesses that selected file and may keep a local copy for its music library and practice features.
  • Musical input: notes, timing, and controller events from the on-screen piano or a connected USB/Bluetooth MIDI keyboard may be processed to play sounds and evaluate practice sessions.
  • Purchases: if an optional Premium purchase is offered, the App uses Google Play Billing to check purchase status, acknowledge purchases, and restore entitlements. The App may receive product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens or related transaction information needed for that purpose. The developer does not directly receive your complete payment-card details.

Based on its currently planned features, the App does not require you to create an account with AlphaSubZero to use its basic lessons or virtual keyboard.

3. How information is used

Information handled within the App is used to operate the piano keyboard and audio engine; display lessons and musical notes; evaluate your timing and accuracy; manage your learning progress and preferences; load MIDI files you select; and recognize Premium entitlements where enabled.

The App's core practice functionality is designed to work without sending your musical performances or imported MIDI compositions to an AlphaSubZero-operated server.

4. Third-party services and purchases

The App may use Google Play services and Google Play Billing to distribute the App and process optional in-app purchases. Google handles payment processing according to its own policies. For more information, see Google Privacy Policy.

The App is not intended to transmit your imported MIDI files or lesson scores to advertising or analytics providers as part of its core functionality. If advertising, analytics, cloud accounts, or additional SDKs are introduced, their use and associated data handling must be described in an updated version of this policy.

5. File access, MIDI devices, and permissions

MIDI files are selected by you using Android's system file picker. The App uses the selected file to enable playback and learning features. A connected USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard may require device connection permission, Bluetooth-related access, or an operating-system pairing step. These permissions are used for musical input and device connectivity.

The current training feature set does not require access to your camera, contacts, or location to perform piano lessons. Permission requests may differ by Android version and connected-device type.

6. Local storage, retention, and deletion

Locally stored learning progress, settings, and imported musical files remain on your device until you remove them using available in-app controls, clear the App's data in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Some Android backup or restoration behavior may be managed by your device and Google account settings.

Clearing App data or uninstalling may permanently remove local lesson results and imported files. It does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase; purchase records and refund handling are managed by Google Play. An eligible Premium entitlement can be restored through Google Play when the App supports that function.

The App currently does not provide a separate AlphaSubZero account registration service, so there is no App-specific online account to delete. If online accounts are introduced later, this policy and the account deletion process will be updated.

7. Security

The App uses Android platform mechanisms for locally stored application data, selected-file access, and connected devices. Payments, where offered, are handled through Google Play. While reasonable care is taken in implementing these features, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

Piano Tiles Academy is intended for musical learning. The App does not require users to provide personal profile information to access its basic lessons. Parents or guardians should supervise purchases and the connection or import of external content where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the App adds new functions, changes service providers, or changes how information is handled. Updated text and its effective date will be published on this page.

10. Privacy inquiries
